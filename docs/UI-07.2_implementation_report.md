# UI-07.2 — Map Cartography Refinement Implementation Report

**Status:** Completed  
**Release Target:** StormOps Themed Cartography & Section Cleanup  
**Git Commit Target:** `fix: refine themed map cartography`  
**Dependencies:** UI-01 through UI-07.1  

---

## 1. Map Heading Change

- **Removed:** The previous section title `"SPATIAL CONTEXT"` and subtitle appendage `"· Evidence"`.
- **Implemented:** The section heading is now strictly:
  `LOCATION MAP`
  Rendered in uppercase with a `Compass` icon, bold monospace font, and themed primary accent color (`var(--accent-primary)`).
- **Subtitle Simplification:** Removed the verbose marketing-like description `" — Geographic orientation & official advisory coverage"`. The subtitle now concisely communicates geographic hierarchy:
  `[Location] · [Admin1 / State] · [Country]` (e.g., `Kolkata · West Bengal · India`).

---

## 2. Footer Removal in Single-Location Mode

- **Single-Location Mode:** The previous redundant bottom legend (`● Selected location`) was **completely removed**. The selected location is already self-evident from the focal beacon, localized aura, adjacent weather label, and header breadcrumb. The map canvas now terminates cleanly at the bottom rounded border without visual clutter.
- **Comparison Mode Exception:** In comparison mode (`locationB` present), a concise, informative legend is rendered below the canvas:
  `● A · Kolkata` and `● B · Howrah` (or Mumbai), clearly distinguishing the two points.
- **Advisory Legend Exception:** When official advisory polygons/markers are active and shown, `▱ Official advisory` is cleanly displayed.

---

## 3. Vandal Cartographic Refinement

- **Problem in UI-07.1:** The previous filter utilized `sepia(100%)`, which converted all colors into a single monochrome sepia channel before rotating into red. This flattened the entire map into uniform red/maroon, coloring water and all road classes identically.
- **Solution:** Replaced the heavy monochrome tint with a calibrated multi-channel filter aligned with Atheris's proven reference hierarchy:
  `invert(100%) hue-rotate(170deg) sepia(18%) saturate(1.25) brightness(0.70) contrast(1.25)`
  - **Atmosphere over Flattening:** Red is restricted to an atmospheric undertone and highway accent, not an opaque wash over the entire map.
  - **Base:** Deep near-black charcoal/slate base (`#080305`).
  - **Atmospheric Vignette:** Reduced radial burgundy gradient (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.06)` to `rgba(159, 18, 57, 0.12)`) with subtle edge shadow.

---

## 4. Midnight Cartographic Refinement

- **Problem in UI-07.1:** The previous Midnight filter applied `sepia(85%)`, resulting in an overpowering, uniform purple blanket across land, water, and roads.
- **Solution:** Shifted to a balanced violet-slate cartographic hierarchy:
  `invert(100%) hue-rotate(200deg) sepia(18%) saturate(1.25) brightness(0.68) contrast(1.25)`
  - **Base:** Deep near-black obsidian-charcoal base (`#06020c`).
  - **Atmosphere:** Subtle deep plum radial vignette (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.06)` to `rgba(76, 29, 149, 0.14)`).
  - **Focal Point:** Starlight amber focal anchor dot (`#fbbf24`) with golden halo popping distinctly against the dark slate background.

---

## 5. Atheris Preservation (Quality Reference Bar)

- **Preservation:** The Atheris map was explicitly identified as the reference quality standard and was strictly preserved without regression:
  `invert(100%) hue-rotate(185deg) brightness(0.68) contrast(1.28) saturate(1.10)`
- **Atmosphere:** Cool cyan/blue radial vignette (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.07)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.22)`).
- **Focal Point:** Electric sky blue (`#38bdf8`) with cyan halo.

---

## 6. Light Preservation

- **Preservation:** Natural daylight OpenStreetMap cartography (`filter: none`) preserved.
- **Container:** Warm neutral slate (`#f8fafc`).
- **Focal Point:** Forest emerald (`#059669`).

## 7. Road Hierarchy Treatment

By removing `sepia(100%)` and controlling saturation, natural raster contrast curves were restored across all dark themes:
- **Major Highways & Arterials (NH16, SH1, Belghoria Expressway):** High-contrast, bright controlled lines (coral/warm red in Vandal, warm violet in Midnight, warm slate/orange in Atheris).
- **Secondary Roads:** Muted, distinguishable mid-tone tracks (dusty rose in Vandal, soft violet in Midnight, cool indigo in Atheris).
- **Minor Local Streets:** Low-contrast dark charcoal lines that provide geographic texture without competing for attention.

---

## 8. Water Feature Treatment

- In UI-07.1, water (River Hooghly) was turned red in Vandal and purple in Midnight due to full-spectrum sepia inversion.
- In UI-07.2, water features now retain their **cool dark blue/slate identity** across all dark themes:
  - In Vandal, the Hooghly is distinct cool dark blue-slate, providing immediate visual contrast against the red road network.
  - In Midnight, water is deep slate-indigo, cleanly separating land masses from rivers.
  - In Atheris, water is deep navy blue.

---

## 9. Geographic Label Treatment

- **Major City Labels (Kolkata, Bidhannagar, Maheshtala, Howrah):** Rendered in crisp, high-contrast light neutral grey/silver text (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Districts and Suburbs:** Muted medium-light neutral grey.
- **Labels are no longer forced into red or purple monochrome:** Text remains neutral and effortless to read.

## 10. Marker & Weather Label Treatment

- **Central Anchor:** 10px circular coordinate dot centered at `(0, 0)` with theme accent and dark border.
- **Beacon Rings:** Concentric pulsing rings highlighting the exact geographic anchor.
- **Adjacent Weather Label:** Positioned at `top: -14px, left: 10px`, containing:
  - Bold monospace temperature numeral (`${temp}°C`).
  - Escaped place name (`locName`).
  - Rain probability badge (`${rain}%`).
